From Pupils to Programmers: The Bauchi Coding Initiative

In 2025, we challenged a group of 15 pupils in Bauchi with a high-level programming curriculum. Many doubted if primary-age children could handle complex logic and syntax.

The result? 100% completion rate. Our students built simple apps to track local water usage and nutritional needs in their own households.

"I used to think computers were magic. Now I know I can build the magic myself." — Amina, 11-year-old participant.
